About Lanka Seagull

A seafood and marine products company based in Adampan, Sri Lanka.

Who we are

Lanka Seagull (Pvt) Ltd is a seafood and marine products company based in Adampan, Sri Lanka. We supply dried sea cucumber, natural sea shells, dried sea snail and sea snail operculum.

Buyers can browse the catalogue, choose quantities by the kilogram or by the piece, and send an order enquiry by WhatsApp or email. Our team then replies to confirm availability, final price and delivery.

Browse products

What we supply

  • Sea Cucumber (14 products): Sandfish Sea Cucumber, Curry Fish Sea Cucumber, Curry Fish (White Grade), Baby Curry Fish, Golden Sandfish Sea Cucumber and 9 more.
  • Sea Shells (14 products): Sea Shells, Patty Shell, Kachchan Shell, Tapasiyem Shell, Gavuri Shell and 9 more.
  • Other Seafood Products (9 products): Dried Sea Snail, Dried Sea Snail (White Grade), Spider Conch Operculum, Murex Operculum, Turbinella Pyrum Shell Operculum (Grade B) and 4 more.

Working with us

We welcome enquiries from wholesalers, importers and business customers. Because details such as grades, pack sizes and shipping depend on each order, we agree them with you directly.
